About me

I'm Kiprotich Isaac Kuboi, based in Kampala. I work as a network engineer and software engineer at MFI Document Solutions Uganda, and I implement and support enterprise applications such as e-learning platforms and document and records management systems (EDRMS).

My strength is turning technical systems into something people can use: onboarding, hands-on training, documentation and support through to handover. I enjoy picking up new tech skills as they emerge.

I'm studying for a BSc in Information Technology at Cavendish University Uganda and hold a Diploma in Infrastructure Management Services (Dean's List, GPA 3.45).
